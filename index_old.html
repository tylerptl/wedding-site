Jarabacoa, Dominican Republic

La Ciudad de las Flores  ·  Valle del Río Yaque del Norte

Nestled in the mountains of the Cordillera Central, Jarabacoa is one of the Dominican Republic's most beautiful hidden gems — lush, cool, and filled with breathtaking waterfalls and natural landscapes. We couldn't imagine a more magical backdrop for our celebration.

Getting There

Travel & Stay

✈️

By Air

Fly into Santiago Cibao (STI) — the closest airport, roughly one hour from Jarabacoa. Las Américas (SDQ) in Santo Domingo is a scenic 2.5-hour drive. We recommend arriving the day before to settle in.

🚗

Ground Transport

Renting a car is the most flexible option — the winding mountain road into Jarabacoa is gorgeous. Private shuttles are available from both airports. We'll share a curated list of trusted drivers closer to the date.

🏡

Where to Stay

From eco-lodges tucked into the jungle to elegant mountain resorts, Jarabacoa has something for every traveler. We've reserved a block for the wedding party at our venue — details coming soon for other guests.

🌡️

What to Expect

June in Jarabacoa is warm but pleasantly cooler than the coast — averaging 72–82 °F (22–28 °C). Pack a light layer for evenings. Dress code is tropical garden formal: think linen, florals, and easy elegance.
